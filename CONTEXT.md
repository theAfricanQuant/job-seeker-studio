# Global Job Seeker Studio — Glossary

## Product locale

The language used by the website interface. English is the launch locale;
French is the next planned locale.

## Document language

The language of a generated CV or cover letter. It should match the selected
vacancy and the candidate's declared ability. A product locale does not imply
that a candidate can truthfully claim professional fluency in that language.

## Market coverage

The job sources and countries for which the product currently returns real,
attributable listings. Global accessibility is not a claim of global market
coverage.

## Candidate evidence

Facts the visitor reviewed and approved from their CV or profile. Tailoring
may prioritize or phrase this evidence; it must not create unsupported claims.
