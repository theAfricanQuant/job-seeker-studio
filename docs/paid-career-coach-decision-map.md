# Paid Career Coach decision map

## #1: What is the paid unit of value?

Type: Grilling

### Question

Should a job seeker buy a time-based subscription, a fixed campaign pack, or
both? This determines limits, payment integration, and whether the product
fits episodic job searches.

### Answer

Deferred until the free pilot has real usage evidence. The launch version has
no payment, paywall, or paid coaching feature. Observe where applicants stop,
which evidence questions they cannot answer, and whether they ask for deeper
tailoring before choosing a paid unit.
