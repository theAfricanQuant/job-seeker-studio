# Field Notes glossary

## Candidate

The person using Field Notes to review their evidence, find roles, and prepare
application documents. A candidate's CV may contain an email address, but it
is profile information rather than a requirement to start the pilot.

## Browser workspace

The private collection of one visitor's profile, uploaded CVs, job cache, and
generated documents. An opaque cookie held by that browser accesses it; it is
not tied to the visitor's CV email address.

## Workspace cookie

An HttpOnly, random browser credential that points to one browser workspace.
It is created automatically, is not an account, and cannot recover a workspace
after browser data is cleared.

## Candidate evidence

Facts the candidate has reviewed and approved. Tailoring may order this
evidence for a job; it may not add unsupported claims.
